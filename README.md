### Hi, I'm Shreya 👋

I make messy research data and half-configured codebases usable 

I'm a computer science student at Vishwakarma University, Pune, and I like the unglamorous
part of research software: the cleaning, the setup, the "it works on my machine" fixes.

**What I can take off your plate**

- **Data cleaning** — pandas pipelines for survey and instrument exports, with a written
  report of every row and value changed.
- **Text preprocessing** — normalisation, tokenisation, stopword removal, lemmatisation,
  driven by a config file you can cite in a methods section.
- **Codebase configuration** — packaging, linting, tests, CI and pre-commit, so a repository
  installs and runs on a fresh machine.
